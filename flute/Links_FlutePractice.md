- *Repetition is the mother of skill [Tony Robbins / Zig Ziglar / Latin proverb]*

My flute practice routine is [here](pov/My_FlutePractice.md). It isn't suitable for serious musicians, but is reasonable
for adult, slow learning hackers like me! :smiley:

The articles below are written for children, university students, online self-learners and those in-between.
You might want to cherry pick the main points or specific points based on where you are in your learning journey.

1. :thumbsup: [The Flute Practice: Tatiana | Slow it Down, Break it Down | 2024](https://theflutepractice.com/blog/slow-it-down-break-it-down/)
   - *Break it down or slow it down!*
   - Formula:
     * Difficulty of a section = The speed of the section X The length of the section
     * The higher the speed and length of the section you are playing the more difficult the section will be. But, by lowering 
       one or both of these parameters, whatever you are playing becomes a lot easier very quickly!
   - How slow is slow? My answer: As slow as you need!

1. [Learn Flute Online: Rebecca Fuller | Create Your Perfect Flute Practice Routine: Steps to Sound Better Every Day | 2025](https://learnfluteonline.com/perfect-flute-practice-routine/)
   - The Power of Warm-Ups: e.g. Long notes | Listen | Analyse your sound
   - Mastering Scales and Arpeggios: Use a metronome | Start slowly | Increase speed
   - Strengthening Note Reading with Exercises and Etudes:  Break the music into small sections | Loop tricky spots | *Accuracy should always come before speed*
   - Expressive Tunes and Solos
   - Conclusion: The Secret Ingredient and Your Next Steps: Start with just 20 to 30 minutes a day | *Acknowledging little victories will keep you motivated*

1. [The Flute Nerd: Alexandra Petropoulos | Building a Sustainable Flute Practice Routine | 2024](https://theflutenerd.com/sustainable-flute-practice-routine/)
   - Find a Schedule That Actually Works for You: *If you can practice for 10-15 minutes 5 days every week, you’ll see improvement faster than if you practice 60 minutes once a week*
   - Practice areas: Warm ups | Tone exercises | Scales | Studies | Repertoire
   - The Role of Breaks and Rest | Reviewing and Adjusting Your Plan

1. [Music Lessons Academy: Vanee | Master Flute Practice: Build Skills Without Burnout Today! | 2026](https://www.musiclessonsacademy.com.au/master-flute-practice-build-skills-without-burnout-today)
   - Content: Setting Up Your Practice Environment for Success | Warm-Up Routines That Prevent Injury and Improve Tone | Technical Skill Development Without Overwhelm |
     Incorporating Repertoire Practice Strategically | Mental Practice and Score Study | Time Management Strategies for Busy Musicians |
     Avoiding Common Practice Pitfalls | Motivation and Goal Setting for Long-Term Success | Health and Wellness in Practice Routines
   - *Research shows that focused, shorter practice sessions often yield better outcomes than marathon sessions*
   - Quality practice involves engaging multiple areas of your brain simultaneously: motor skills for finger movements, auditory processing
     for pitch recognition, and cognitive functions for reading music
   - Before playing through a piece, examine it for technical challenges, musical phrases, and performance considerations.
     *Identify difficult passages and practice them separately before integrating them into the full piece.*
   - Slow practice is like using a magnifying glass; it allows you to examine and perfect details that get lost at faster speeds
   - *One of the biggest mistakes is practicing mistakes.* When you repeatedly play incorrect notes or rhythms, you’re actually training your muscles to make those mistakes.
   - Sustainable practice routines include proper posture, regular breaks, adequate hydration
